# CRM & Admission Pipeline

## Purpose
Manage marketing leads from first enquiry through admission.

## Pipeline
NEW -> CONTACTED -> FOLLOW_UP -> INTERESTED -> COUNSELLING -> APPLICATION -> CONVERTED/ADMITTED.

Also support LOST with a reason. Stages should ultimately be configurable.

## Lead Data
Name, phone, email, interested course, source, campaign, assigned counsellor, stage, priority, next follow-up, notes and consent/preferences where applicable.

## Activities
Calls, messages, meetings, notes, follow-ups, reminders, stage changes and assignments.

## Conversion
Conversion creates/links an admission record without losing CRM history.

## Reports
Leads by source/campaign, counsellor workload, pending follow-ups, stage funnel, lost reasons, conversion rate and admissions attributed to marketing source.

## AI
AI can summarize leads, draft follow-up messages and identify overdue follow-ups. It must not send communications without the user's configured authorization/workflow.
