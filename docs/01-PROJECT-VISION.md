# Project Vision

## Product
RCLD Islampur ERP is a unified digital platform for RCLD Islampur. The current deployment is for one physical centre, while the architecture should avoid blocking future multi-branch expansion.

## Goals
- Digitize admission-to-certificate operations.
- Give offline students a self-service portal.
- Provide a separate online-student LMS experience.
- Manage employees, attendance, breaks, out-times, leave, payroll, tasks and performance.
- Connect fees, payments, expenses, payroll and financial reporting.
- Manage marketing leads and admissions through CRM.
- Provide CMS control over the public website.
- Add permission-aware AI assistance throughout the platform.
- Maintain auditable records and documented restore points.
- Support English, Hindi and Bengali.

## Primary Personas
- Superadmin
- Employees/teachers/admin users with one or more configurable roles
- Offline students
- Online students

Parent/guardian login is out of scope for the initial release.

## Design Direction
The public entry experience and dashboards should be modern, responsive and visually attractive, using a glassmorphism-inspired design without sacrificing accessibility, performance or readability.

## Initial Courses
CCA, DCA, ADCA, CCC, CAO, DWPD, DOM, DCST, DCAP and DHNT.

Course names, duration, eligibility, fees, online fees, syllabus and status are configurable data and must not be hard-coded into business logic.

## Principles
Configuration over hard-coding; least privilege; auditability; modular design; mobile responsiveness; multilingual readiness; accessible UX; financial traceability; AI with human oversight.
