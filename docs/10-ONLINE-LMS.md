# Online Learning Platform

## Separation
Online and offline student accounts are separate by current business requirement. An offline student purchasing online learning creates/uses a separate online account.

## Journey
Register -> Profile -> Browse Course -> Purchase -> Payment -> Enrollment -> Learning Dashboard.

Initial online payment model is one-time purchase.

## Learning Features
Recorded video links, PDFs, notes, downloadable files, assignments, quizzes, doubt questions, announcements, progress tracking, exams and certificates.

## Content Management
Authorized teachers/admins can upload/manage course content according to permissions granted by Superadmin.

## Video
YouTube is the initial video source. Final privacy/access implementation must be validated before launch; application permissions alone cannot guarantee protection of a shareable YouTube URL.

## Progress
Track lesson completion, course progress, assignment/quiz activity and last-access information.

## Student Dashboard
Continue learning, enrolled courses, progress, notices, downloads, assessments, results, certificates and AI tutor.
