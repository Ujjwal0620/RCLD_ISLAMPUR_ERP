# CMS, Multilingual Content, SEO & GEO

## CMS
Manage homepage sections, pages, banners, course content, gallery, notices, FAQs, testimonials, contact information, social links and metadata.

Admins publish only when their Superadmin-granted permission allows it. Maintain draft/published state and revisions for important content.

## Languages
English, Hindi and Bengali. Public content should support translations with a clear fallback strategy.

## SEO
Use server-rendered/indexable public pages where appropriate, descriptive URLs, page metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots controls, Open Graph/social metadata, optimized images, internal linking and performance monitoring.

## Structured Data
Use applicable schema.org structured data such as Organization/EducationalOrganization, Course, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage only where page content legitimately matches the schema.

## GEO / AI Discoverability
Write factual, structured, entity-consistent content; clear course pages; useful FAQs; visible institutional information; semantic headings; strong internal links; and machine-readable structured data. Do not use spammy AI-generated pages or unsupported claims.

## Public Areas
Home, about, courses, admission, online courses, notices, gallery, contact and certificate verification, with final navigation controlled through CMS where appropriate.
